Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our business loan products.

What documents do I need to apply for a business loan?
Most applications require your business tax returns (last 2 years), personal tax returns, bank statements (last 6 months), business financial statements, and a valid government-issued ID. For SBA loans, you may also need a business plan and additional documentation. Our team will guide you through exactly what's needed for your specific loan type.
How long does the approval process take?
Most business loan applications receive an initial decision within 24 hours. Once approved and documents are signed, funding typically occurs within 1-5 business days, depending on the loan type. SBA loans may take longer (2-4 weeks) due to additional government processing requirements.
What credit score do I need to qualify?
We consider multiple factors beyond just credit score, including business revenue, time in business, and industry. Generally, a personal credit score of 640+ and business credit score of 160+ (D&B PAYDEX) are preferred. However, we work with businesses across the credit spectrum and can often find solutions for those with lower scores.
Are there any prepayment penalties?
Most of our business loans have no prepayment penalties, allowing you to pay off your loan early without additional fees. Some SBA loans may have a small prepayment penalty during the first few years, which will be clearly disclosed in your loan agreement.
Can I use the loan for any business purpose?
Yes, our term loans and lines of credit can be used for virtually any legitimate business purpose, including expansion, working capital, inventory, marketing, hiring, or refinancing existing debt. Equipment financing is specifically for purchasing equipment, and SBA loans have specific eligible uses outlined by the SBA.
Do I need to provide collateral?
It depends on the loan type and amount. Equipment financing uses the equipment itself as collateral. SBA loans typically require collateral for larger amounts. For term loans under $250K, we often offer unsecured options for qualified borrowers. Our team will discuss the best structure for your specific situation.
