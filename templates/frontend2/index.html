Manual installation guide

Choose your device below for step-by-step instructions

Install on iPhone or iPad

Use Safari browser — other browsers don't support installation on iOS

1

Open Safari browser

Launch the Safari app on your iPhone or iPad. Note: Chrome, Firefox, and other browsers do not support PWA installation on iOS.

2

Navigate to our website

Go to pinnaclepbonline.com in the Safari address bar and wait for the page to fully load.

3

Tap the Share button

Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari (the square with an upward arrow). On iPad, it's at the top of the screen.

4

Select "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down the share menu and tap "Add to Home Screen". You can rename the app if you'd like.

5

Tap "Add" to confirm

Tap "Add" in the top-right corner. The PinnaclePB icon will now appear on your home screen — tap it to launch the app!

Install on Android

Works with Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet, and most modern browsers

1

Open your browser

Launch Chrome (recommended) or any modern browser on your Android device.

2

Visit our website

Navigate to pinnaclepbonline.com and wait for the page to fully load.

3

Tap the browser menu

Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner of your browser.

4

Look for install prompt

You may see an "Install app" banner at the top of the page. If so, tap it and skip to step 6. Otherwise, continue to step 5.

5

Select install option

In the menu, tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen". The wording may vary slightly by browser.

6

Confirm installation

Tap "Install" in the confirmation dialog. The app will be added to your home screen and app drawer automatically.

Install on Windows

Works with Microsoft Edge, Chrome, and Brave browsers

1

Open your browser

Launch Microsoft Edge (recommended) or Chrome on your Windows PC.

2

Visit our website

Go to pinnaclepbonline.com and wait for the page to fully load.

3

Look for install icon

Look for the app install icon (a monitor with a down arrow ↓) in the right side of the address bar. Click it.

4

Alternative: Use browser menu

If you don't see the icon, click the three dots in the top-right → Apps → "Install this site as an app".

5

Click "Install"

In the popup dialog, click "Install". The app will open in its own window.

6

Pin to taskbar (optional)

Right-click the app icon in your taskbar and select "Pin to taskbar" for quick access anytime.

6

Launch the app

Click the PinnaclePB icon in your Dock to launch the app. It runs in its own window, just like a native app!

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the app

What is a Progressive Web App (PWA)?

A Progressive Web App is a website that can be installed on your device like a native app. It works offline, sends push notifications, and provides a full-screen experience — all without downloading from an app store.

Does the app take up storage space?

Very little! The PinnaclePB app uses less than 5MB of storage — compared to 100-300MB for traditional apps. It intelligently caches only what you need.

Is the app secure?

Absolutely. The app uses the same top-grade 256-bit encryption as our website, supports biometric login (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint), and includes real-time fraud monitoring.

How do I update the app?

Updates happen automatically! Whenever you open the app, it checks for updates in the background. You'll always have the latest features and security patches without lifting a finger.
