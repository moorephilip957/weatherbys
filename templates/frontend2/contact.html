Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services and support.

How do I open a new account with PinnaclePB?
Opening an account is simple and takes just 5 minutes. Click the "Open an Account" button on our homepage, fill out the application form, complete our quick KYC verification by uploading your ID, and you're all set. You'll receive your account details and can start enjoying our premium services.
What are your customer support hours?
Our standard support hours are Monday-Friday 8AM-8PM and Saturday 9AM-5PM. However, our 24/7 emergency support line is always available for account security issues, lost/stolen cards, and urgent matters. You can also access our mobile app and online services 24/7.
How do I report a lost or stolen card?
If your card is lost or stolen, immediately call our 24/7 emergency line at +1 (800) 123-4567 or use the "Freeze Card" feature in your mobile app. Our fraud team will issue a replacement card within 2-3 business days at no charge.
Are my deposits insured?
Yes, all deposits at PinnaclePB are FDIC insured up to $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank. Your money is completely safe and protected by federal regulations.
How do I apply for a loan or mortgage?
You can apply for loans and mortgages directly through our website or mobile app. Simply navigate to the Loans section, choose your loan type, and complete the application. Our team will review your application and get back to you within 24-48 hours. You can also schedule a consultation with one of our loan specialists.
What security measures do you have in place?
We employ bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption, multi-factor authentication, biometric login (Face ID/fingerprint), real-time fraud monitoring, and AI-powered anomaly detection. All transactions are monitored 24/7, and we never share your personal information with unauthorized third parties.
