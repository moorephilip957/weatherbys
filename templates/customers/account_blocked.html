Frequently asked questions

Why was my account restricted?
Accounts are typically restricted when we detect unusual activity that doesn't match your typical usage patterns. This could include large transactions, logins from new locations, or multiple failed login attempts. This is a security measure to protect your funds and personal information.
How long will my account be restricted?
Most account restrictions are resolved within 1-2 business hours after you complete the verification steps. In some cases, additional review may be required, which could take up to 24-48 hours. We'll notify you via email as soon as your account is restored.
Can I still receive money?
Yes, you can still receive money into your account while it's restricted. However, you won't be able to send money, make purchases, or withdraw funds until the restriction is lifted.
Is my money safe?
Absolutely. Your funds are completely safe and secure. The restriction is a precautionary measure to protect your account. All your money remains in your account and is FDIC insured up to $250,000.
What documents do I need to verify my identity?
You'll need a valid government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, or national ID card) and a recent selfie. Make sure the photos are clear and well-lit. The name on your ID must match the name on your account.

Security reminder

Hampdens Bank will never ask for your password, PIN, or verification codes via email, phone, or text. If you receive suspicious communications claiming to be from us, please report them to our security team immediately.
